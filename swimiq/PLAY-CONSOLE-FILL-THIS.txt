SwimIQ is the competitive swimming app built for club athletes, parents, and coaches who want clarity between practice and race day.

• Visual progress, streaks, and SwimIQ score

Athlete Passport
• Recruiting-ready athlete snapshot
• Power Index and top times

• Cut-to-College timeline toward the next cut and target schools

AI that coaches, not just scores
• Video Lab uploads for race and technique clips
• AI stroke analysis with clear priorities (Elite)
• Race Intelligence warm-up, nutrition, and meet-day checklist
• AI Dryland Coach tied to your latest technique needs

For families and coaches
• Parent Race Pulse — plain-English weekly progress
• Coach preview access for team evaluation
• Web + Android so athletes can train anywhere

Plans

• Pro — official times, passport, recruiting tools, dryland
• Elite — AI video analysis, Race Intelligence, recruiting insights

Built by a swim family for swimmers chasing cuts, PRs, and college interest.
